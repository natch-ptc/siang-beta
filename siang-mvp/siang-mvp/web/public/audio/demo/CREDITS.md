# Demo sounds

These files play on the six booth demo artists' works (see supabase/migrations/0018_demo_artists.sql).
All come from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded on 2026-10-28. Each was cut to the length listed, faded in and out, and encoded as MP3.
Three works reuse sounds from ../examples/ (pencil, wind chime, paper).
